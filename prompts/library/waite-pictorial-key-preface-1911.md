The Tarot embodies symbolical presentations of universal ideas, behind which lie all the implicits of the human mind, and it is in this sense that they contain secret doctrine, which is the realization by the few of truths embedded in the consciousness of all. The cards are a work of art — I say this carefully — and a work of doctrinal art, which is something rarer.

The designs of the present pack have been prepared under my supervision and in accordance with the knowledge that I possess concerning the true meanings. They differ in many material respects from anything which has appeared previously in works on the Tarot. The pictures of the greater trumps have been entirely redrawn, and I believe, for the first time in the history of this subject, every card in the pack has received full pictorial treatment. This was possible because the artist who gave form to my design — Miss Pamela Colman Smith, whose name deserves to stand beside the work she has produced — brought to her task not only skill and trained imagination, but an occult sympathy that is, in my experience, uncommon among professional draughtswomen.

I must add only this: the meanings here given belong to the exoteric tradition of cartomancy, which is not the same as the esoteric doctrine of the symbols. The student who has received real initiation will look at these cards differently — will see in the Fool, for example, not a figure of folly, but a figure of spiritual detachment of the highest order. The surface meanings are offered in good faith; the deeper meanings are for those who have eyes already opened.
