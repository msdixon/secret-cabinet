The word that was made flesh was made fresh
It is the new, the new test .. the new tester, the test-tester-testament
The testament new
Words, words, words
Made fresh, made again
The recreate, the recreation . . .

—

Jesus said, "Let the Negro bury the Negro." At least that is what he said in the original Greek Version of the New Testament. But according to genesis C and G are interchangeable and for this reason the worlds of Jesus also reads, Let the Negro bury the Necro."... In the present day language , the sentence just quoted reads: "Let the dead bury the dead."...
