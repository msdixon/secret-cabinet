O ye my persecutors! Ye banned me on the pretence that I am waging a war [jihad] against you. Indeed ye are right because I am really combatting for the Countenance of the Lord. But I am waging my Jihad through Knowledge [ulūm] and Fearing the Lord [taqwā], as [a humble] subject of God and the servant of His Prophet; and the Lord who oversees everything may assuredly bear witness thereof ... While others hold material weapons to be feared, my two weapons are [knowledge] and [worship]; and this is surely my way of fighting.
