The astral light is the life-principle of our planet. It is the storehouse of all forms and all occurrences; the mirror of everything that has existed, exists, or will exist; the living source of prophecy, clairvoyance, and vision. It does not vanish; it preserves all things, including the intimate records of every human life — every thought left its trace, every deed its mark, every passion its impress upon this subtle medium.

It is what Éliphas Lévi called the great Magical Agent — the light of the Astral world, or Inner Light of nature — differentiated from the Akasa only by its lower and more physical qualities. He was right to name it so, and right to connect it with the universal will, though he understood it, as the Kabbalist he was, largely through the lens of personal magnetism and individual force. Our traditions are older. In the East, this same principle was known to the Rishis who wrote the Upanishads, and to the Tantrikas who worked with Prana. Lévi borrowed — we do not begrudge him that — but he borrowed partially. What he calls the will of the magician acting upon the Astral is, in our teaching, only the most surface expression of what is in truth a cosmic memory, a living record through which the trained initiate moves as through a library.

Every adept who has learned to read the Astral Light reads, in effect, the whole history of the world.
