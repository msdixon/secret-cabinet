Know that the imagination is not nonexistent, even though it has no real external existence. Do you not see how, when you are asleep, you imagine things which, within the context of that state, have a real existence for you, and how you act in accordance with them? Waking you say they were not real — but this is a claim that requires examination. The waking state has its own veil.

The whole cosmos is imagination within imagination. What you call Real is the Divine Essence appearing to Itself through the forms It creates. The mystic who knows this does not need to abolish forms to reach unity; she moves through forms as through rooms in a palace whose walls are made of light. The form is the sign; the sign points; the pointing reaches the signified. There is no shortcut that bypasses the sign, only fools imagine they have found one.

This is why I say: the one who worships God in a particular form worships truly, for God is in that form. And the one who says there is nothing but God, and therefore refuses all forms, has — paradoxically — made his refusal into a new form, and worships that. The imagination cannot be escaped. It can only be known for what it is.

Shu'ayb said to his people: what I forbid you, I do not wish to do differently myself. This is the mark of realisation: the inward and outward have become one fabric, and the teacher does not possess a secret exemption from what he teaches. The sage lives inside the wisdom she speaks.
