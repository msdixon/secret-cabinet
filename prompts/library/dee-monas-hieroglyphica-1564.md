Most Sacred and Most Mighty King: I have long observed that the study of hieroglyphic monas — that is, the single, unified symbol which I have here contrived — is of a threefold utility. First, it perfects the mind of the student by forcing it to move from particular to universal and back again without losing the thread. Second, it constitutes a kind of key to natural philosophy, by which I mean a method of reading nature's own signs — the same signs which God wrote upon the face of the world before any human alphabet existed. Third, and most extraordinarily, it repays an almost unlimited amount of meditation; I have sat with it for weeks on end and it has not exhausted itself.

I wrote this work in twelve days. I say this not to boast but to indicate the nature of the knowledge involved — it is not discursive, not built up laboriously, but arrives entire or not at all. The symbol itself was not invented by me; it was found. I found it the way one finds a word one had forgotten, by becoming quiet enough to hear it.

The sign unifies the Sun and the Moon, the elements, the four points of the compass, the ten numerals, the three worlds. This is not metaphor. The sign contains all of this structurally, as a key contains the shape of the lock it was made to open. Those who think I am speaking of correspondences have not yet understood me. I am speaking of identities.

I dedicate it to Your Majesty because only a sovereign can properly receive what transcends all particular sovereignty.
