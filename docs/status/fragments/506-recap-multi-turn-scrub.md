Extended the `#506` recap mechanism ([PR #536](https://github.com/msdixon/secret-cabinet/pull/536)) from a single-slot "last turn" replay into a bounded, steppable turn history: `⏮ Prev turn` walks backward through already-spoken live turns one at a time, `Next turn ⏭` walks forward again, both reusing the same `renderWitnessBlock` seam. Settles the issue's open design question in favor of discrete previous-turn jumps over continuous Roku-style rewind — `Voice.speak()` has no persisted/replayable audio object, so there's no audio timeline to scrub, only turn boundaries to jump between (button wording deliberately avoids a time-based "back 15s" convention for the same reason).
