A 2026-08-26 design critique of #348 (PR #440, which had shipped a status button mirroring "Play as" next to Convene the Lodge) found that button visually diluted the two real convene actions and reused the additive-action dashed-border convention for a non-action. Removed it, folding its live-state readout into a new "You" chip in members-grid (same chip styling as any unseated member) whose click reveals the mode/member/custom-identity fields inline in place of the old collapsed details panel. [#348](https://github.com/msdixon/secret-cabinet/issues/348), [PR #454](https://github.com/msdixon/secret-cabinet/pull/454).
