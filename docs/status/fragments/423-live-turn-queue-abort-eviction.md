Fixed the live-turn queue wedging forever after a mid-turn generation error ([#423](https://github.com/msdixon/secret-cabinet/issues/423)): a thrown mid-stream error left the in-flight turn stuck at the front of `witness.js`'s `#400` queue with no `block`, and `advanceLiveTurnQueue`'s guard then blocked everything behind it — including a successful retry — until a full `resetLiveTurnQueue()`. Confirmed via a jsdom reproduction against the real module, which also surfaced a compounding DOM collision in room mode. `witness.js` now exports `liveAbortTurn()`, called from `app.js`'s `abort()`, which evicts the stuck turn and cleans up its orphaned typing entry.
