[#350](https://github.com/msdixon/secret-cabinet/issues/350) accent half of #338's split shipped: a hand-set `voiceAccent` layers on top of gender/demeanor, filtered gender → accent → demeanor (accent wins the fallback tiebreak — a wrong accent is more jarring than a slightly-off demeanor). The premade 21-voice pool turned out to be 16 american/4 british/1 australian with zero German/French/Italian/Irish/Russian/Spanish coverage (verified live against `GET /v1/voices`), so `FALLBACK_VOICE_IDS` grew by 14 voices pulled from ElevenLabs' broader shared-voice library, each verified live via `/v1/text-to-speech`. 29 of 38 members tagged where the record is clear; 9 left unset (medieval Arab/Andalusian/Sufi figures, mixed-heritage cases) rather than guessed. Crowley now resolves to a British voice instead of the generically American one that motivated the issue — confirmed live end-to-end, not just tagged. Web Speech stays deliberately accent-unaware: no browser locale exists for "English spoken with a German/French accent," and forcing a non-English-locale voice would mispronounce the text outright.
