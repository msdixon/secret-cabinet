Added the confessional beat type (#579), the last part of the 2026-09-13 citation-as-combat redesign: the director can propose one member who has already spoken tonight to step apart and work through the texts at length (at most one per passage, gated on budget headroom). The confessional goes into the displayed record with its own bubble and card treatment, but it's left out of `conversationHistory`, so the room never hears it and its length doesn't pull later turns longer (#561). It hasn't been evaluated against real sessions yet. [#579](https://github.com/msdixon/secret-cabinet/issues/579)
