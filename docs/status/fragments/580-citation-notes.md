First step of "the researcher can act" ([#580](https://github.com/msdixon/secret-cabinet/issues/580), v1): an owner can attach a private note to a single citation on `/bibliography`, stored on the session as `citationNotes` and keyed by the citation's position in the raw beat capture so it survives Verify Citations. Notes are visible and editable only to the session's owner (stripped from `GET /api/sessions/:id` for other readers); v2 (pin a moment), cross-session per-work notes, and steer-not-splinter remain open.
