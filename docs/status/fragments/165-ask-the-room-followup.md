Interject evolved into "Ask the room" (#165 Phase 1): you pick one member and ask a direct question, and they answer once in voice via a new `POST /api/followup` (single turn, thinking off, no director/disposition/citation pass). The exchange joins the record as an interjection-kind "Mid-session Chat: <name> → <member>" segment — so it appears in the transcript, exports and annotations, and is restored on reload — and enters conversation history, but writes no disposition or residue (residue is global per member, #595); `/api/interject` itself is unchanged.
