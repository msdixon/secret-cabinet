Fixed the lull ("Continue"/"Let it end") prompt appearing while dialogue or audio was still playing ([#475](https://github.com/msdixon/secret-cabinet/issues/475)): `runLullLoop()` in `app.js` rendered the lull as soon as `streamPost()` resolved, which is only the *server* finishing generation — `witness.js`'s `#400` live-turn queue was often still pacing that same text/audio onto the stage. Added `waitForLiveQueueDrain()` to `witness.js`, resolved from the same three spots that already bring the queue to empty, and gated the lull's appearance on it — the identical signal `collapseStage()` already used on the dismissal side. Verified live against a real voice-on convene session: the lull correctly stayed withheld for the full duration of a long paced passage and appeared only once the queue actually drained.
