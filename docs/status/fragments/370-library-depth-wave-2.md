[#370](https://github.com/msdixon/secret-cabinet/issues/370) shipped 35a's first depth-after-coverage round: a second, different-genre authored entry for 6 of the library's 38 covered members, on axis A (register depth) per Rachel's pick over the issue's other three candidate axes. Each pairs the member's existing formal/published text with a conversational one — a letter, diary, or epistle — chosen where a public-domain source was easy to find and independently verify, several deliberately landing on themes the original entry already used for graph connective tissue (axis B as tiebreaker): William Blake's 1803 Felpham letter to Butts (echoes his own "contraries" and Catherine Blake's "felpham"/"correspondence"); John Dee's 1596 diary entry, a book-vision dream (echoes his Monas Hieroglyphica's "hieroglyph"); Teresa of Ávila's 1576 letter to her brother, funny and domestic against The Interior Castle's mysticism; Swedenborg's Journal of Dreams (raw dream-record against Heaven and Hell's polished empirical testimony); Jacob Böhme's Third Epistle, autobiographical about writing Aurora under persecution, against Signatura Rerum's cosmology; and H. P. Blavatsky's first letter to Sinnett, unguarded and funny against Isis Unveiled's treatise voice. Held for Rachel rather than curated this round: Crowley and Waite (autobiographies with unresolved US copyright-renewal status), Yeats and Jung (letter collections still under active copyright/estate control), and Ibn Khaldun (no public-domain English translation of his autobiography found).

A second author-credited entry surfaced a real gap: #187's `loadVoiceExemplar` only ever reads the *first* author match in `library.json`, so a second entry did nothing for the exemplar system it was meant to serve without a code change. Added `loadSecondaryVoiceExemplars` (`src/library.js`) and extended `pipeline-speaker.js`'s prompt builder to inject the primary exemplar at full weight plus any secondary entries as smaller, explicitly-supplementary "tone-tuning" passages — weighted lighter, correcting or sharpening the primary rather than competing with it. `test/library.test.js`'s old one-entry-per-author guard relaxed to a two-entries-per-author ceiling. 44 library entries, 6 with a secondary voice register, 965 total tests.
