First cut of front-door starters ([#623](https://github.com/msdixon/secret-cabinet/issues/623)): ten curated provocations (`prompts/library/starters.json`, validated against the library) appear under the resting stage, each seating a 2–3 member cast, loading the excerpt and convening live in one click, plus a "Surprise me" button; they hide once a meeting begins. "Show me a sitting" ships as a `sitting` data hook linking to the reading room (null until a session is chosen), so the on-stage replay and "convene your own variation" fork are still open.
