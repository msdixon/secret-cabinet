This track was explicitly paused for autonomous pickup because it needed a human-confirmed source; Rachel supplied one — Deja Whitehouse, "'Mercury Is in a Very Ape-Like Mood': Frieda Harris's Perception of Thelema," *Aries* 21.1 (2021): 125–152, an open-access (CC BY 4.0) article quoting Harris's unpublished letters to Crowley. Harris has no standalone published prose of her own, so unlike prior entries this one combines three short quotes (11 Dec [1939], 22 Oct 1940, 8 Nov 1944, ~195 words total) into a single entry, the same multi-quote pattern the Scholem diaries entry used — chosen because together they show a more distinct voice than any one alone: wry domestic-magical friction, the "plumber-demons" anecdote that gives the article its title, and a closing alchemical self-assessment. Filed `fair-use` rather than `public-domain`: the letters are unpublished archival material (Syracuse SCRC / Warburg Institute Yorke Collection) still under her estate's copyright, reproduced here via Whitehouse's CC-BY article.
